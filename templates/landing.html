Privacy & security

Built for thoughts you would not put in a spreadsheet.

The design minimizes what exists to steal, reset, or quietly reuse. It also tells you where the privacy boundary actually is.

Read the security design ↗
01

Per-user encryption

Conversation and memory content is encrypted with a key unlocked by your passkey or recovery key.

02

No password reset

There is no operator-held master recovery key. Lose every passkey and your recovery key, and the account cannot be restored.

03

Encrypted storage

SQLCipher protects the database at rest, while sensitive content receives an additional application-level encryption layer.

04

An honest AI boundary

The model must read what you send to respond. Requests use zero-retention provider routing by default; this is policy protection, not magic encryption.

Open source

Open source, in the open.

Read the code, inspect the security decisions, run it yourself, or improve it. IndividuateAI is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later, so hosted modifications stay available to their users too.
