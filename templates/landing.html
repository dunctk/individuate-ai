Privacy & security

Your private notes need strong protection.

We protect saved data and explain where that protection ends.

Read how security works ↗
01

Your passkey unlocks your data

Each account has its own encryption key. Your passkey or recovery key unlocks it.

02

No reset back door

We do not hold a master recovery key. If you lose every passkey and your recovery key, no one can restore your account.

03

Saved data is encrypted twice

Sensitive content is encrypted for your account. The whole database is encrypted again.

04

The AI reads each message

The AI provider must read a message to answer it. By default, we use providers that promise not to store or train on requests.
